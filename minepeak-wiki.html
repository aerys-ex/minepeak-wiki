Welcome to the MinePeak Wiki!

MinePeak is a Minecraft multiplayer server with Survival, PvP, and minigame experiences. Players can build, trade, complete quests, join clans, compete in events, and play a range of fast-paced game modes. This wiki documents MinePeak's history, community, features, and notable players.

About the server

MinePeak publicly launched on 14 November 2025. Its official server address is play.minepeak.org. The server supports versions from Minecraft 1.8 through the latest supported release.[1]

MinePeak offers a mix of long-term progression modes and quick competitive games. Survival players can build, farm, trade, complete quests, participate in airdrops, and climb leaderboards. The server also runs events and maintains rules, moderation, and anti-cheat systems.[1]

Getting started

  1. Open Minecraft and select Multiplayer.
  2. Add a server using the address play.minepeak.org.
  3. Join the network and choose a game mode.
  4. New Survival players should protect their land before building anything valuable, then begin with quests, gathering resources, and learning the economy.

For the official join guide, see How to Play on MinePeak.

Survival, economy, and progression

Survival is MinePeak's main long-term progression mode. Players can earn resources and money through farming, trading, player shops, quests, events, and airdrops. Land protection is an important early step for players who want to keep their builds and items secure.

Coin Market

The Coin Market was introduced in the Spring 2026 reset for Survival and Lifesteal. It lets players list valuable items, inventories, or in-game money for coins.[2]

CommandPurpose
/mkOpen the Coin Market.
/mk sell [price]List the held item for coins.
/mk sellinv [price] [name]List an inventory with a name and price.
/mk sellmoney [amount] [price]List an amount of in-game money.
/mk search [name]Search the market for an item.
/mk [username]View a player's active listings.

Players can set a shop name and Discord tag in Market settings. Listings with more sales rank higher in the Market.[2]

Quests and Coin Shop

Quests were revamped in the April 2026 reset. The Farmer, Alchemist, and Armorer quest lines are available through /quest. The Coin Shop can be opened with /coinshop and includes items such as totems, Driller's Shards, tridents, and spears.[2]

Clans

Clans let players work together, coordinate resources, and compete as groups. The April 2026 update expanded clan tools:

CommandPurpose
/c logsView the clan event log.
/c logs chatView clan-chat messages in the log.
/c logs memberView member joins and leaves.
/c logs kickView clan kicks.
/c logs balanceView clan balance withdrawals and top-ups.
/c statsView clan members and their statistics.

The log commands support page numbers, for example /c logs 10.[2]

Events and support

MinePeak runs events, including themed quests, airdrops, tournaments, and activity rewards. Event schedules and registration are available through /eventsmenu. Players can submit bugs with /bugreport.[1], [2]

For current news, rules, support, and announcements, use the official MinePeak website and Discord.
